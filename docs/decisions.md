| 2026-11-23 | E0-S1 | Open for E0-S4/security: `npm audit` reports `zod` 3.x (high, memory exhaustion on oversized collections) through A2UI and CopilotKit, and older `dompurify` 3.4.11 (moderate XSS plus lows) through `@a2ui/markdown-it` 0.2.2. `glob`/`minimatch` (moderate) are Angular CLI tooling only and are not shipped. | 2026-11-23 | E0-S1 | Strict spec validation is written out in `src/app/catalog/catalog.ts` (`STRICT_SPEC_VALIDATION`: no unknown elements, dangling references, orphans or missing root) and always passed as `processorOptions.validationConfig`. Supersedes the `STRICT_VALIDATION` entry below. | `@a2ui/web_core` 0.14 does not export `STRICT_VALIDATION`. | 2026-11-23 | E0-S1 | `package.json` pins `vite` 7.3.8 (dev dependency plus `overrides`). | Angular 21.2 builds with Vite 7.3.8. Not a `--force`/`--legacy-peer-deps` workaround: the resolved tree is the one Angular itself uses. |
| 2026-11-23 | E0-S1 | Known risk of the A2UI 0.14.0 pin: `@a2ui/angular` 0.14.0 depends on `web_core` ^0.15.0, but its required peer `@a2ui/markdown-it` 0.2.2 accepts only `web_core` ^0.14.0 (no `markdown-it` release accepts 0.15). npm installs both: the renderer runs on 0.15.0, the markdown plugin and our imports on 0.14.0. | Clean install and the render test pass, but schemas and types come from two copies.
